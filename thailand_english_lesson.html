📚 Part 1

Essential Phrases

The most important phrases every traveller needs. Learn these first!

01 · Greeting
Hello! / Hi!
Привет!
02 · Polite
Thank you very much!
Большое спасибо!
03 · Restaurant
Can I have this, please?
Можно мне это, пожалуйста?
Point at the menu!
04 · Very Important!
Not spicy, please!
Не острое, пожалуйста!
🌶️ Thai food can be VERY hot!
05 · Shopping
How much does it cost?
Сколько это стоит?
06 · Navigation
Where is the hotel / beach / toilet?
Где находится...?
07 · Emergency
I need help, please!
Мне нужна помощь!
08 · Bargaining
Can you make it cheaper?
Можно подешевле?
Thais love to bargain!
09 · Wi-Fi
Do you have Wi-Fi? What's the password?
У вас есть Wi-Fi? Какой пароль?
10 · Goodbye
Goodbye! Have a nice day!
До свидания! Хорошего дня!
🚀 Part 2

Advanced Phrases

Ready for the next level? These will make you sound like a pro!

Health & Safety
Does this dish contain peanuts? I'm allergic.
В блюде есть арахис? У меня аллергия.
Restaurant
Could you recommend something popular with locals?
Порекомендуете что-то популярное у местных?
Bargaining
I saw the same thing at another stall for half the price.
Я видел то же самое вдвое дешевле.
Best bargaining argument!
Transport
How long does it take to get there?
Сколько времени добираться туда?
Hotel
The air conditioning in my room isn't working properly.
Кондиционер в моём номере плохо работает.
Social
Your country is absolutely beautiful — I'd love to come back someday.
Ваша страна невероятно красива!
Locals will love this! 😊
🛠️ Part 3

Universal Phrases

These 10 phrases work in almost any situation. Memorise them!

Didn't understand?
Sorry, could you say that again more slowly?
Не могли бы вы повторить помедленнее?
Can't remember?
Could you write it down for me?
Не могли бы вы написать это?
Checking price
Just to confirm — is everything included in the price?
Просто уточню — всё включено в цену?
Shops & Markets
No, thank you, I'm just looking.
Нет, спасибо, я просто смотрю.
Saves you from pushy sellers!
Lost?
Could you help me? I'm a bit lost.
Не могли бы вы помочь? Я немного заблудился.
Payment
Do you accept credit cards or cash only?
Вы принимаете карты или только наличные?
Meeting people
Nice to meet you! I'm from Russia.
Приятно познакомиться! Я из России.
💡 Magic phrase
Could you...?
Не могли бы вы...?
Polite in ANY situation — use it everywhere!

🌏 Part 5

7 Fascinating Facts About Thailand

Learn about the country whose language you're speaking!

01
🐘 The Land of White Elephants
White elephants are considered sacred and belong to the King by law. Giving one as a "gift" was historically used to ruin enemies — so expensive to care for, it could bankrupt anyone. That's where the phrase "a white elephant gift" comes from!
02
😄 13 Types of Smiles
Thailand is called "The Land of Smiles" — but Thai people actually have 13 different smiles, each with its own meaning. Only 3 of them mean happiness! There are smiles for sadness, disagreement, embarrassment, and even bad intentions. So not every smile means "I'm happy"!
03
🙅 Never Touch Someone's Head
The head is the most sacred part of the body in Thai culture. Touching anyone's head — even a child's — is deeply disrespectful. On the other hand, pointing your feet at a person or a temple is also a serious insult, as feet are considered the lowest part of the body.
04
🌊 Over 1,400 Islands
Thailand has over 1,400 islands. Some are world-famous, but hundreds are completely uninhabited jungle. Several Hollywood movies were filmed there — including "The Beach" with Leonardo DiCaprio. Most tourists only ever visit a handful!
05
🕌 40,000 Buddhist Temples
Thailand has approximately 40,000 Buddhist temples — more than there are McDonald's in the entire world! The most famous, Wat Phra Kaew in Bangkok, is home to the legendary Emerald Buddha — which is actually made of jade, not emerald!
06
🥊 Muay Thai: The Art of 8 Limbs
Muay Thai has existed for over 2,000 years and was originally a fighting art for the Thai army. Unlike regular boxing, it allows knees, elbows, fists AND feet — giving it the nickname "the art of eight limbs". Many Thai children start training as young as 6 or 7 years old!
07
🛶 Floating Markets
In Thailand, there are markets built on the water — vendors sell food, fruit and souvenirs directly from boats. The most famous, Damnoen Saduak near Bangkok, is over 100 years old. Historically, rivers and canals were the main "roads" of the country, so all trade happened on water. Today it's one of the most photogenic spots in all of Thailand!
